## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Profile → Edit account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, posts, and drinking history.

**How do I remove a photo I posted?**
Profile → Your sessions → tap the drink → Remove photo. The check-in stays; the photo is permanently deleted.

**Who can see my posts?**
Only buddies you have accepted. There are no public profiles.

**Why can't I find a drink?**
Try a rough spelling — search is forgiving. If it's genuinely missing, you can add it (with its brewery and style) from the post screen.
