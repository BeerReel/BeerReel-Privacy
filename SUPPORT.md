# BeerReel Support

Need help with BeerReel, found a bug, or have a question about your account or data?

**Email us:** [beerreelapp@gmail.com](mailto:beerreelapp@gmail.com)

We aim to reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: You → Account details → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, posts, and drinking history. Drinks, breweries and pubs you added stay in the shared catalogue, and events you attended carry on for the other attendees — though your own photos leave with you.

**How do I remove a photo I posted?**
Sessions → tap the drink → tap the ✕ on the photo → Save. The drink stays; the photo is permanently deleted.

**Who can see my posts?**
Your buddies see your posts in the feed. Anyone at an event with you sees the ones you post there, and keeps them in the event album permanently. Someone who only knows your handle sees your photo, name and handle, and nothing else. The full picture is in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html) under "Who can see your content".

**Who can see my event photos?**
Everyone at the event, and an event is joined with a six-character code — so anyone who was given that code and used it. The album is permanent and outlives the party. If you want your photos out of it, leave the event: that withdraws them from everyone else's album too.

**Why am I on the map?**
The Map of Legends shows whoever has logged the most drinks at each pub over the last 90 days. If that is you, your handle, name and profile photo are shown to every BeerReel user. Drink somewhere else and it passes.

**Where does the pub list come from?**
Pubs are imported from OpenStreetMap and checked against Food Standards Agency hygiene data, so most of them are there before anyone types anything. If one is missing, wrong or closed, you can add or report it in the app. Credits and licences are in the [Privacy Policy](./privacy.html).

**Why can't I find a drink?**
Try a rough spelling — search is forgiving. If it's genuinely missing, you can add it (with its brewery and style) from the post screen. New entries are reviewed before they join the shared catalogue.
