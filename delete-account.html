Delete your BeerReel account

Last updated: 27 September 2026

You can delete your BeerReel account yourself, from inside the app, at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

In the app

  • Open BeerReel and go to the You tab.
  • Tap Edit account.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  • Confirm. You are signed out and the account is gone.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email beerreelapp@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for it to be deleted. We will confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, display name, handle, profile photo, password hash, taste profile and push notification token.
  • Every drink you logged, with its photo, rating, notes and the pub it was logged at, and every session those drinks belonged to.
  • Your buddy connections, pending requests, blocks, cheers, tags, reposts and invite code.

Any event you were hosting ends at the moment of deletion. Other people's content is untouched: a drink a buddy logged that tagged you stays on their account, without the tag, and pubs or drinks you suggested to the shared catalogue remain in it, no longer linked to you.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Safety records made about other accounts (reports and moderation decisions) are kept for the accounts they concern. Database backups exist only for disaster recovery, are overwritten on a rolling basis, and deleted accounts are never restored from them.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • A photo — You → Your sessions → tap the drink → Remove photo. The drink stays; the photo is permanently deleted.
  • A drink — You → Your sessions → tap the drink → Delete drink.
  • A buddy — You → Buddies → tap the buddy → Remove.
